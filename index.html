About

W.R.Roach writes Christian nonfiction under W.R.Roach Ministries. His books take the words beneath the English of the King James Bible and set them down in plain language, for people in grief, in recovery, behind bars, or simply worn out.

Many of the books are built for groups. Companion workbooks and facilitator’s guides let a church, a recovery program, or a chaplain lead them, with room on the page for honest answers.
